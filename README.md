## Non-negotiable rules honored here

- Truthfulness over impressiveness. No invented experience, metrics, achievements, certifications, or results.
- The reference repo's fact-status system is respected: `PLANNED` is never promoted to `COMPLETED`, `TO_VERIFY` is never promoted to `VERIFIED`, `EXCLUDED` stays excluded.
- Professional identity is **not pre-decided** — it is derived from market evidence vs. the subject's demonstrable background.
- Every material claim carries a source and a confidence level; weak evidence is never presented as fact.
