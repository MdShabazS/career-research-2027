# LinkedIn — Fresher-Realistic Benchmark

Calibration of the elite patterns for a final-year student. What is realistic and what over-reaches.

## 1. Realistic headline for a student
- `[REC, High]` Include: current identity (final-year ECE), the target role, 3–4 named technologies, and the internship affiliation if allowed. Keep it specific and honest — no "Senior", no invented specialties.
- `[INFER, High]` Example shape (not final copy): `Embedded Software · Embedded C, ESP32/STM32, on-device CV · Final-year ECE · Intern @ <employer>`.

## 2. About section for a fresher
- `[REC, High]` Hook (what you build across embedded + on-device intelligence) → what you actually did at internships (app-side at iHelp; high-level at Nokia) → 2–3 real project proofs with honest numbers (VisionPay ~93% *reported*; ESP32 BCM FSM) → what you're learning next (RTOS, protocol depth) → a light call to action. First person, short sentences.

## 3. What freshers get wrong
- `[INFER, High]` Copy-pasting the resume; empty "Open to opportunities" with no proof; listing 30+ endorsable skills; claiming AI/ML ownership beyond the truth; a headline of just "Student" or just "Software Engineer"; inconsistent dates vs. the resume.

## 4. Realistic proof for a student
- `[FACT, High]` Featured = GitHub repos + (later) the portfolio; internships as the credibility anchor; genuine leadership (IEEE roles) and activities (IEEE SPACE 2026 as *participant*, not award). (careerbldr, resumeworded; cross-checked with reference repo.)

## 5. Consistency mandate
- `[REC, High]` The reference-repo audit found the candidate's LinkedIn currently uses a **software-first** headline while resume/GitHub use embedded-first, and lists "REST APIs" as a skill it does not hold. Fix both: one positioning across channels; remove unheld skills.
